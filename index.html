About Champions Sports Academy

Building Discipline, Strength & Fearless Character

Located at Barnwal Dharmshala, Banakata, Gopalganj, Champions Sports Academy (मार्शल आर्ट और फिटनेस सेंटर) is Gopalganj’s premier sports training hub. Dedicated to shaping children, teens, and adults into agile athletes and confident martial artists through rigorous yet safe coaching.

State-Level & National Coaching: Regular sparring, tournament participation, and recognized belt examinations.

Traditional & Modern Disciplines: Comprehensive curriculum combining Korean Taekwondo, Yogic conditioning, Kabaddi, and acrobatic martial arts stunts.

Lead Instructor Sumit Sharma: Personalized mentoring focusing on endurance, discipline, agility, and mental tenacity.
